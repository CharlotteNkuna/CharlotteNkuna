## About Me

I am an **aspiring Full-Stack Developer** from Johannesburg, South Africa.

My journey into software engineering has been driven by curiosity, hands-on projects, and a desire to understand how real-world applications are designed and built.

I enjoy working across both the **frontend and backend**, with a particular interest in backend development and building complete full-stack applications.

I have experience working with **Java, Spring Boot, Express.js, Node.js, Angular, React, TypeScript, JavaScript, PostgreSQL, HTML and CSS**.

I am currently deepening my knowledge of **Java and Spring Boot**, with a focus on REST APIs, databases, authentication, application architecture, and connecting backend services with modern frontend applications.

I am also expanding my programming knowledge with **Python**.

I believe the best way to learn software engineering is by building. Every project gives me an opportunity to strengthen my problem-solving skills, understand software engineering principles, and grow as a developer.
